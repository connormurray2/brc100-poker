1 · Start your wallet

You play through a BRC-100 wallet running on your own machine. It holds your key, and this page never sees it. The wallet also holds the secrets that keep your hole cards private, which is why the game needs it rather than a browser extension.

No key yet? Run go run ./cmd/keygen -out secrets/player.key first, and back up both the key and the database — the database is the wallet.

-max-pot lets your wallet sign without asking, up to that many satoshis per pot. Without it, every hand waits for you to type y in the terminal — which is fine for one hand and impractical for a session. Your wallet still refuses anything above the limit, and still checks every settlement against what it expects to be paid.

Keep this tab open while you play. The table cannot reach your wallet directly — it runs elsewhere, and 127.0.0.1 there would mean the server — so this page carries deal traffic between them. Everything is signed end to end, so the page cannot read or alter it.

Your cards are dealt dealerlessly: every seat holds a secret for your card positions and discloses it only to you. Nothing else — including this server — can read your hand.
